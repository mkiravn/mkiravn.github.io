About

I'm a British/Danish/Italian PhD student in the Novembre Group at the University of Chicago, now in my fifth year of the Human Genetics program. My research is about how relatedness is structured in space: measuring how environments are shared between relatives, and what that means for the genetic architecture of complex traits.

Before Chicago I was in the Racimo lab at the University of Copenhagen, where I did an MSc in Bioinformatics and worked on spatial population genetics, mostly in ancient DNA. Before that I studied Natural Sciences at Cambridge.
